# Space is already strange enough

## Stars make their own light

The Sun is a star. Nuclear fusion in its core releases energy; the Moon shines mainly by reflecting sunlight.

## Sound needs something to travel through

Sound waves need a medium such as air. The near-vacuum between worlds does not carry an ordinary conversation. Radio waves can travel through vacuum.

## Gravity has not gone away

Astronauts in orbit float because they and their spacecraft are falling together around Earth. They are not beyond the reach of gravity.
