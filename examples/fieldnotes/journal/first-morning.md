# The morning the path disappeared beneath a very ordinary amount of fog

## At the gate

The gate was open. A bead of water hung from its lowest hinge, catching the pale light whenever the branches moved. Beyond it, the path seemed to finish after six steps.

I waited for the woodland to become more specific. It declined.

## Along the path

The notebook stayed dry beneath my coat. Each time I stopped, sounds that had seemed distant moved closer: leaves brushing leaves, a loose branch, water falling into a puddle already full of water.

> Observation: visibility improves considerably when you stop polishing your glasses with a wet sleeve.

### A small clearing

There was no dramatic discovery. Just a fallen log, a patch of moss, and enough room to sit without being on the path. Some entries earn their place by being uneventful.

## Back at the gate

The same bead of water had gone. I wrote that down too.
