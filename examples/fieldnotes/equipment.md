# What fits in a coat pocket

- A pencil that writes in the cold.
- A small notebook with a firm cover.
- A cloth kept separately from the wet gloves.

## Record an observation

1. Write the place and time.
2. Describe what you noticed before deciding what it means.
3. Leave room for a correction.

**Useful:** a spare pencil. **Less useful:** five identical photographs of your thumb.
