# Forty-eight unhurried hours in North Quay

## Day one

1. Arrive at the harbour.
2. Walk uphill until the boats look small.
3. Find the bakery with the blue door.

## Day two

The coastal path begins behind the old signal house. Turn back whenever the view feels sufficient. This is a holiday, not a completion percentage.

| Stop | Time to allow | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbour | An hour | Watch the ropes, not your phone |
| Signal house | Half a morning | Bring a warm layer |
| Bakery | Flexible | The second pastry is research |
