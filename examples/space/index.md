# The Almost Reliable Space Handbook

Welcome, passenger. Your destination is enormous, mostly empty, and unlikely to offer a refund.

The facts section describes the real universe. The travel desk is fiction: its booking policies, hotels, and sentient luggage are entirely invented.

## Before you float away

- Read [a few actual space facts](./facts/).
- Visit the [travel desk](./travel/) for questionable itineraries.
- Consult [lost property](./lost-property.md) if your suitcase has achieved escape velocity.

> Please remain inside the spacecraft until the universe has come to a complete stop.
