# A quiet weekend on the Moon, with only minor administrative complications

## Arrival

Present your imaginary passport to the crater concierge. If nobody answers, appreciate the excellent acoustic insulation.

### Check-in

Your room includes one window, several stars, and no complimentary atmosphere.

## Excursions

Visit the Museum of Things We Forgot to Pack. Its largest exhibit is a toothbrush.

## Arrival

Returning guests must repeat the arrival procedure. The concierge has a very short geological memory.
